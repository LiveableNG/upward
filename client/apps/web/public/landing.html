Your Questions, Answered

Is my financial data secure?
Upward uses 256-bit bank-level encryption. We only verify the transaction data you choose to share, and we never have direct access to your login credentials or bank account funds.
Does my landlord need to be on Upward?
You can build your Payment Consistency score even if your landlord isn't registered. However, for a complete Property Care score, your landlord will need to verify the condition of the space.
How does this help me get a mortgage?
Most banks in Nigeria lack historical rental data. Upward provides a certified Rent Passport™ which proves your financial discipline and ability to handle long-term housing payments.
