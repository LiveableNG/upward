Your questions, answered

Simple, transparent, and built around your rent.

What is Upward?

Upward turns rent payments and rental behaviour into verified rental history, an Upward Score, and access to rewards, savings tools and housing finance opportunities.

Does my landlord need to be on Upward?

You can build payment consistency history even if your landlord isn't registered. Some property-care information may require landlord verification.

Is Upward free?

Creating an account and starting to build your Upward Score is free. Specific financial products may have their own terms.

How can this help with a mortgage?

Upward creates a certified rental history that can help participating mortgage institutions better understand your payment behaviour and housing affordability.

Is my data secure?

Upward is designed to protect the financial and rental information you choose to share, and to use it only for the services you authorise.
