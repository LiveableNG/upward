FAQ

Questions before you apply.

Do I have to choose between Property Management and Brokerage?+

No. Every student learns both. Property Management is the ongoing relationship; Brokerage is deal-based. Specialization comes later.

Do I need to already own property or work in real estate?+

No. The programme is designed for people entering or growing in the industry. Fit, drive and readiness are assessed in the application.

How much time do I need?+

6–8 hours every week across live learning, practical work, building and market activity.

What does Upward Certified mean?+

It is intended as a training and assessment standard, rather than a certificate of attendance.

Is the ₦10m+ figure guaranteed?+

No. It is an aspirational business target, not a guaranteed outcome. Results depend on effort, client acquisition, portfolio size, pricing and market conditions.

What is the ₦5,000 application fee?+

It is credited toward the ₦200,000 programme fee if admitted, and refunded if you do not qualify to join the class.

Is the programme online or in-person?+

Hybrid, weighted toward onsite training. Practical learning is intended to happen in person in the student's city.

Does everyone receive post-graduation business support?+

No. Standout graduates may be invited into a selective deeper relationship with Upward, including support such as advertising, access to customers, introductions and continued business advice.
