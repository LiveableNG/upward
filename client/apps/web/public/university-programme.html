Learn. Build. Launch. Then grow.

A practical 10-week programme starting October 31, 2026 built around doing the work - not sitting through lectures and waiting for a certificate. Property Management, Brokerage and the business skills to turn both into a career or a business.

Classes Commence: October 31, 2026 · Hybrid (Lagos, Abuja, PH, Oyo)
Programme Outcome

Don't just learn. Launch.

Upward University moves students from learning into the market fast. By Week 4, your professional identity, offer and initial market activity should be ready to launch.

Weeks 1–2

Design

Choose your market, customer, offer and business model.

Week 3

Build

Business identity, WhatsApp Business, landing page, proposal and pricing.

Week 4

Launch

Go commercially live and begin outreach to real prospects.

Weeks 5–10

Acquire → Earn → Grow

Win clients, deliver, earn and build your portfolio.

What You Learn

Three skills that work together.

Property Management

Landlord relationships, rent collection, maintenance, tenant relations and portfolio growth.

Brokerage

Finding clients, sourcing opportunities, marketing, viewings, negotiation and closing.

Business

Positioning, pricing, prospecting, client acquisition and building a sustainable professional identity.

By The End

What you'll be able to do.

Ten concrete outcomes the programme is built around: not just topics covered, but things you can actually do.

  • ✓ Manage properties professionally.
  • ✓ Acquire and retain landlords and tenants.
  • ✓ Market and let properties.
  • ✓ Handle rent collection, inspections, and maintenance.
  • ✓ Use technology to improve property operations.
  • ✓ Communicate and negotiate professionally.
  • ✓ Understand the legal and regulatory environment.
  • ✓ Build a professional network and personal brand.
  • ✓ Generate commercial opportunities: have at least one landlord in your portfolio.
  • ✓ Eventually, build a property management or brokerage practice.
Full Curriculum

Week by week.

Every week pairs a Property Management & Brokerage topic with a business-building lesson or activity, so you're never just learning theory in isolation.

Property Management & Brokerage

Program Orientation

Excellence mindset; possibilities; dreaming big; program expectations; dos and don'ts; code of conduct; professionalism; participation and commitment.

Business Lesson / Activity

Standing Out as a Business or Career Professional in 2026

Panel session with Q&A. Business activity: start lead generation with 20 potential clients.

Speaker: Azubuike Ezenwoke
Property Management & Brokerage

Property Management & Brokerage Fundamentals

Property management vs. brokerage; understanding the problems landlords have; role of property managers and real estate agents; Nigerian real estate industry; types of property; property lifecycle; brokerage models; agency relationships; income and commission structures; career paths; PM and brokerage fundamentals.

Business Lesson / Activity

Business Model Canvas

Choose a target market, define the type of property/landlord to serve, select a business model, define the service offer, and draft a simple business proposal.

Property Management & Brokerage

Landlord Acquisition, Onboarding & Property Sourcing

Winning and prospecting landlords; property sourcing; prospecting property owners; landlord qualification; off-market opportunities; property verification; onboarding; property documentation; building inventory; setting expectations; management agreements; landlord communication; client relationship management.

Business Lesson / Activity

Personal Branding & Business Credibility

Professional identity; LinkedIn; social media; credibility; thought leadership; reputation management. Business activity: choose a business brand name. Build a credible public profile, publish useful property-management content and improve the landlord-facing brochure and proposal.

Property Management & Brokerage

Client & Tenant Acquisition, Qualification & Screening

Prospecting; lead generation; finding tenants and clients; buyer/tenant profiling; client needs analysis; lead and tenant qualification; KYC; tenant screening; rental applications; tenant risk assessment; client and tenant communication; tenant onboarding; CRM.

Business Lesson / Activity

Professional Communication & Business Foundation

Email etiquette; WhatsApp communication; professional writing; client conversations; active listening. Business activity: finalise service packages and pricing, prepare a proposal/management agreement, review prospect list, register a business name and set up social presence.

Property Management & Brokerage

Property Marketing, Letting & Lead Management

Property listings; listing creation; property photography; listing descriptions; pricing; social media and listing platforms; digital marketing; lead generation; viewing coordination; enquiry and lead management; prospect qualification; property viewings; converting enquiries into tenants.

Business Lesson / Activity

Go To Market: Digital Marketing, Referrals & Brokerage Growth

WhatsApp and social media marketing; content creation; creating an online reputation. Business activity: create a content plan, and develop partnerships with relevant property professionals and service providers.

Property Management & Brokerage

Tenancy, Rent Management, Negotiation & Closing

Rental structures in Nigeria; rent collection; agency and management fees; rent reminders; tenancy agreements; renewals; arrears management; notices; rent payment tracking; dispute management; rent and price negotiation; commission negotiation; objection handling; closing techniques.

Business Lesson / Activity

Setting Up Your Back Office

Client reporting templates, core SOPs, compliance/documentation checklists, and a simple operating dashboard for the business. Visit / pitch to your first 10 leads.

Property Management & Brokerage

Property Inspections, Viewings & Client Experience

Move-in and move-out inspections; property condition assessment; condition reports; inventory; identifying defects; preventive inspections; inspection documentation and reporting; scheduling and conducting viewings; site etiquette; client qualification; follow-up; converting viewings into tenants.

Business Lesson / Activity

Financial Management & Business Operations

Personal finance for professionals; property cash flow; management fees; commissions; budgeting; basic financial reporting. Business activity and field work: set up financial tracking and sign up your first client.

Property Management & Brokerage

Add-on Partner Services — Valuation, Maintenance & Facilities Management

Property valuation; maintenance requests; emergency response; work orders; contractor management; preventive maintenance; service charges; budgeting; property preservation; landlord/tenant maintenance responsibilities.

Business Lesson / Activity

Introduction to Valuation

Panel session with NIESV representatives. Build referral partnership(s) for valuation and facility management. Optional: develop a basic FM value offering.

Property Management & Brokerage

Reporting, Documentation, Transaction Management & Compliance

Property records; offer letters; tenancy documentation; agreements; transaction records; receipts; due diligence; financial reports; inspection and incident reports; document management; audit trails; data management; client reporting; privacy and confidentiality.

Business Lesson / Activity

Business Advisory Session

Review client acquisition progress, review your value proposition, and pitch your next 10 potential clients — with advisors in the room.

Property Management & Brokerage

Building a Tech-Driven Property Management Business

PropTech fundamentals; digital rent collection; property management software; digital maintenance; automated communication; dashboards; property data; tenant data; AI and automation; PM business models; management fees; acquiring portfolios; service design; client retention; team structure; portfolio growth; profitability; building a PM brand.

Business Lesson / Activity

Legal, Ethical Practice & Business Growth

Professional ethics; client protection; fraud prevention; regulatory awareness; conflicts of interest; professional conduct. Business activity: integrate technology, legal/ethical controls, and growth systems into the business; review portfolio and revenue; define 90-day growth targets; and present a final Property Management Business Growth Plan.

Property Management & Brokerage

Case Studies: Critical Thinking, Problem Solving & Offer Design

Spotting discrepancies; analysing property issues; risk assessment; decision-making; landlord/tenant conflicts; maintenance emergencies.
