Frequently Asked Questions

Is this information session free to attend?
Yes, 100% free. There is no charge to attend the information and Q&A session. We host these sessions to ensure every candidate understands the commitment and opportunities before applying.
Do I need prior real estate experience to join?
No previous experience is required. The programme is designed for beginners as well as active agents looking to transition into structured property management and recurring retainer income.
How will I receive the meeting link?
Once you reserve your spot, we will send the live Zoom/Google Meet link and calendar invite directly to your WhatsApp number and email address before the session starts.
What if I cannot attend at these times?
You can still register and indicate your interest. We will share session summaries and upcoming alternate dates via WhatsApp. You can also contact our team directly.
