Frequently Asked Questions

Everything that doesn't fit neatly into the main story — but still deserves a straight, plain-English answer.

The Programme
Do I have to choose between Property Management and Brokerage?

No. Every student learns both. Property Management is the ongoing relationship — rent collection, maintenance, tenant relations. Brokerage is deal-based — finding clients and closing transactions. See the full curriculum for how they fit together.

Do I need to already own property or work in real estate?

No. We're not looking for existing property owners or industry veterans — we're looking for people with drive and communication skills. This is assessed as part of your application.

Is training online, in-person, or hybrid?

Hybrid, with classroom training on weekends — so it fits around a job or studies without requiring you to quit anything to start.

Who's actually teaching this?

Practitioners currently active in Nigerian real estate, not full-time lecturers reading from a syllabus. See the full list of lecturers & mentors.

Income & Outcomes
Is the ₦10m+ income figure guaranteed?

No. It's an aspirational target, not a guaranteed outcome. Results depend on individual effort, client acquisition, and market conditions.

Is starting to earn by Day 60 guaranteed?

No individual outcome can be guaranteed — but it's a realistic milestone for students who complete the programme and engage with the apprenticeship placement, which gives you access to real client units from early on.

What if I want a job, not my own business?

That's a real path here too. The skills, credential and network apply just as directly to employment at an established property company as they do to starting your own practice.

Applying & Paying
Do I have to pay to attend the information session?

No. It's free, with no obligation. Application and payment only come after — once you've had every question answered.

What does it cost, and are there scholarships?

See the full breakdown on our Fees & Scholarships page — including the registration fee, programme fee, scholarship stack, and payment plan.
