    Here's What You'll Learn at the Webinar

    No fluff or theory — just the practical mechanics of how property management works in Nigeria.

    What Property Managers Actually Do

    Day-to-day responsibilities: tenant relations, rent collections, maintenance coordination, and landlord reporting.

    How Property Managers Earn Money

    Standard management fee models, commission percentages, and how managers scale their monthly earnings.

    How managing properties for landlords differs from investing, and how to offer services with zero upfront capital.

    The Skills You Need to Succeed
